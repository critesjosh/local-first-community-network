# Child Safety Standards for Local Community Network

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2024
**Last Updated:** November 9, 2024

## Our Commitment to Child Safety

Local Community Network is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We strictly prohibit any content, behavior, or activity that exploits or endangers children.

## 1. Published Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

### 1.1 Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following content and conduct are strictly prohibited on our platform:

#### Absolutely Prohibited:

- **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)**: Any visual depiction or content involving minors in sexual or sexually suggestive situations
- **Child Grooming**: Any attempt to establish an emotional connection with a minor to lower inhibitions for sexual abuse
- **Child Sexualization**: Content that sexualizes minors in any way, including through images, text, or other media
- **Solicitation**: Any solicitation of minors for sexual purposes
- **Child Trafficking**: Content that facilitates or promotes child trafficking or exploitation
- **Inappropriate Contact**: Adults attempting to establish inappropriate contact with minors
- **Sextortion**: Threatening or coercing minors into providing sexual content
- **Child Sex Tourism**: Content that promotes or facilitates travel for the purpose of sexual exploitation of children

#### Additional Protections:

- Age-inappropriate content shared with minors
- Sharing of personal information of minors without proper consent
- Attempts to contact minors for purposes unrelated to legitimate community activities
- Content that normalizes or trivializes child abuse

### 1.2 Age Restrictions

- Our app is intended for users aged 13 and older
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
- Users who misrepresent their age will have their accounts terminated
- Special protections apply for users aged 13-17 (see Section 1.3)

### 1.3 Special Protections for Minors (Ages 13-17)

For users between 13-17 years old, we implement additional safeguards:

- **Restricted Visibility**: Minor accounts have enhanced privacy settings by default
- **Connection Limitations**: Connections require mutual consent and proximity verification
- **Content Monitoring**: Enhanced monitoring of interactions involving minor accounts
- **Parental Controls**: Parents/guardians can request oversight of minor accounts
- **Educational Resources**: Age-appropriate safety information and resources

## 2. In-App User Feedback and Reporting Mechanism

### 2.1 How to Report Safety Concerns

We provide multiple ways for users to report child safety concerns:

#### Within the App:

1. **Report Button**: Every post, event, and user profile has a "Report" button
2. **Report Categories**: Users can select from specific categories including:
   - Child safety concern
   - CSAM
   - Inappropriate contact with a minor
   - Grooming behavior
   - Other safety issues
3. **Anonymous Reporting**: Reports can be submitted anonymously
4. **Priority Processing**: All child safety reports are flagged for immediate review

#### Direct Contact:

- **Child Safety Email**: childsafety@adjacentpossible.dev
- **24/7 Monitoring**: Child safety reports are monitored continuously

### 2.2 What Happens When You Report

1. **Immediate Action**: Reports are immediately flagged and reviewed by our safety team
2. **Content Preservation**: Reported content is preserved for investigation and potential law enforcement cooperation
3. **User Protection**: We may immediately suspend accounts involved in serious violations
4. **Investigation**: Our team investigates all reports thoroughly
5. **Follow-up**: Reporters receive confirmation that action has been taken (within privacy constraints)

## 3. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) Policies

### 3.1 Detection and Prevention

We implement multiple layers of protection:

- **Proactive Monitoring**: Automated systems and manual review processes, where possible
- **User Reporting**: Community-driven reporting mechanism
- **Encryption Considerations**: While we use end-to-end encryption for user privacy, we retain the ability to respond to valid legal requests and CSAM reports

### 3.2 Cooperation with Law Enforcement

We fully cooperate with law enforcement investigations:

- Provide preserved evidence when legally required
- Work with NCMEC and international child safety organizations
- Participate in industry initiatives to combat CSAM

## 6. Content Moderation and Enforcement

### 6.1 Enforcement Actions

Violations result in immediate action:

#### Minor Violations:

- Warning to user
- Content removal
- Temporary account suspension

#### Serious Violations:

- Immediate account suspension
- Content preservation for investigation
- Report to law enforcement
- Permanent ban
- Device-level blocking

#### CSAM Violations:

- Immediate permanent ban
- Full cooperation with law enforcement
- Report to NCMEC CyberTipline
- Preservation of all evidence
- Prevention of account recreation

### 6.3 Prevention Measures

We implement proactive measures:

- **Education**: Safety tips and resources for users
- **Behavioral Analysis**: Detection of suspicious patterns
- **Community Guidelines**: Clear standards for acceptable behavior
- **Parental Resources**: Information for parents about keeping children safe

## 7. Privacy Considerations

While we maintain strong privacy protections through end-to-end encryption:

- We can and will respond to valid legal requests
- CSAM reports override privacy protections
- We preserve metadata that can assist investigations
- We balance user privacy with child safety imperatives
- Our encryption does not prevent us from taking action on reports
